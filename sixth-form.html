Why Kingsley Sixth Form?

More than A-levels — a complete preparation for life

At Kingsley Sixth Form, every student is supported by a personal tutor who ensures they not only excel academically, but do so through building and maintaining positive mental health and a growth mindset. Academic ambition and personal wellbeing go hand in hand.

Students study three A-level or BTEC subjects plus an Extended Project Qualification, with access to an additional 44 courses via Kingsley Online — our unique collaboration with Greene's Tutorial College in Oxford. This gives Kingsley Sixth Formers one of the broadest subject offers of any school in the South West.

The Expansive Curriculum continues into Sixth Form, allowing students to maintain their passions in sport, performing arts, outdoor education, leadership, service and volunteering — all of which strengthen university applications significantly.

Flagship Programme

The Top Universities Programme

The Top Universities Programme is Kingsley's flagship Sixth Form offer for students aspiring to Oxbridge, Russell Group universities, Medicine, Veterinary Medicine or Engineering. Delivered in partnership with Greene's Tutorial College in Oxford, it provides tailored support and preparation for the most competitive university pathways.

Lower Sixth students undergo a comprehensive UCAS induction, attend the Exeter University Fair and benefit from dedicated personal statement preparation and university visit opportunities. Specialist advisers guide every student from course selection through to offer.

A limited number of Top Universities Scholarships — worth up to 85% off published fees — are available for students on this pathway.

Kingsley Online

44 additional subjects — taught live

Kingsley Online is a unique collaboration between Kingsley School and Greene's Tutorial College in Oxford. Students may choose one of their three A-level or BTEC subjects to be studied online, with a live tutor responsible for their progress and providing instant feedback.

This means Kingsley Sixth Formers have access to over 44 additional A-level courses — one of the broadest subject offers in independent education — while their remaining subjects are delivered in person at Kingsley with full access to the Expansive Curriculum.
