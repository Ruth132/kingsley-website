What is CEA?

The Continuity of Education Allowance explained

The Continuity of Education Allowance (CEA) is a financial benefit available to Service personnel to help fund boarding school education for their children. It is designed to provide stability and continuity for children whose families are subject to frequent postings.

As an approved CEA school, Kingsley can receive CEA funding directly. Families should contact the Joint Personnel Administration Centre (JPAC) to confirm their eligibility and begin the application process. Our admissions team is happy to guide you through the paperwork.
