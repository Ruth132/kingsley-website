Welcome to Prep

A small school family with big ambitions

Kingsley Prep has a distinctive small-school family feel, where children of all ages know and support each other during lessons, playtimes and activities. Classes are kept deliberately small, with plenty of support in every classroom. Children with a wide range of abilities and interests all flourish here — and every achievement, however small, is celebrated.

Parents often describe the Prep as "a second family" — a place where children are treated with genuine care, where joy and kindness fill the corridors, and where calm purpose underpins each day. Here, children feel safe, valued and confident enough to grow.

"There is no other place where all of my children — with different needs — could be supported and celebrated in the same school."
Kingsley Prep Parent

Inclusive Excellence

📚
Strong Academic Foundation

A rich, broad curriculum across all core subjects, delivered by specialist teachers in small classes where every child receives real individual attention and support.

💜
Wellbeing at the Centre

Holders of the nationally recognised Wellbeing Award for Schools. Through initiatives such as Girls on Board, pupils build trust, navigate friendships and develop emotional resilience.

🌊
Coastal Curriculum

Our North Devon location shapes everything. From beach trips and outdoor education to the Surf Academy, children learn with and from their remarkable natural surroundings.

🧩
SEND Excellence

CReSTeD accredited — the only school in Devon and Cornwall so recognised. High-quality SEND provision is embedded in daily classroom practice, with the Connect Centre for more complex needs.

🎭
Rich Activities Programme

Science, robotics, coding, reading, art, cartoon creating, computer animation, knitting and sewing clubs — something for every child, whatever their interest.

🎓
Pathway to Senior School

Children move seamlessly into the Senior School with strong foundations, familiar faces and the confidence that comes from seven years of being truly known and supported.

New Head of Prep — September 2026

Mrs Samantha Hammond joins as Head of Prep

From September 2026, Kingsley Prep welcomes Mrs Samantha Hammond as our new Head of Prep School. Previously serving as Director of Inclusive Education, Sam brings deep expertise in SEND provision, inclusive practice and the kind of warm, relational leadership that defines Kingsley Prep.

Sam's appointment reflects Kingsley's continued commitment to inclusive excellence — ensuring that every child, regardless of learning profile or starting point, is supported to flourish academically, socially and personally.
