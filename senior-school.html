Our Approach

Where every student exceeds expectations

Kingsley is a fully inclusive, proudly non-selective school — yet our students consistently exceed national expectations. How? Because we believe the foundation of academic success is a young person who feels safe, known and genuinely supported. Small classes, expert teaching and outstanding SEND provision ensure no student is left behind.

Our curriculum is distinctive: a Core Curriculum of 45-minute morning lessons delivers a rigorous academic programme, while the Expansive Curriculum — delivered in longer afternoon sessions — is where passions ignite and learning truly comes alive.

And yes — every student surfs during PE lessons.

Pastoral Care & Wellbeing

Mental health is the foundation of success

At Kingsley, mental health and wellbeing aren't a bolt-on — they're the foundation on which everything else is built. Our pastoral team knows every student individually. Form tutors, houseparents and specialist wellbeing staff work together to ensure every young person feels supported, understood and never alone.

Our Resilience Tightrope framework — delivered through the Outdoor Education pathway — gives students a clear, structured way to build emotional resilience with demonstrable outcomes. We believe the best academic results come from young people who feel well.
