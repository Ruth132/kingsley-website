FC Rise & Kingsley Sport

Elite sport, without compromise

FC Rise is Kingsley's elite football academy, offering full-time professional football development alongside a first-class education. Students train with qualified coaches daily, play in competitive fixtures, and benefit from analysis, strength and conditioning, and a genuine pathway into the game.

Beyond football, Kingsley fields competitive teams across rugby, netball, swimming, athletics and more. Our Sporting Excellence pathway within the Expansive Curriculum gives every student the time, coaching and resource to pursue sport seriously — whatever their discipline.

Sport & Academics

No student has to choose

At Kingsley, the timetable is built around the student. Athletes attending FC Rise or national training camps are fully supported to catch up on missed work. Small class sizes mean teachers know every student, and pastoral staff ensure no one falls through the gaps during intensive sporting periods.

Our record speaks for itself — students competing at elite sporting level consistently achieve strong academic results, because Kingsley's approach ensures both are taken equally seriously.
