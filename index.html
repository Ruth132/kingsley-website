Our Ethos

Achieving success through positive mental health and wellbeing

At Kingsley, we believe the foundation of every achievement — academic, personal, sporting — is a young person who feels genuinely known and supported. Our core values of Appreciate, Be Active, Connect, Give and Learn run through every part of school life.

We are inspired by Charles Kingsley, a North Devon author who believed in the transformative power of a holistic education. We combine modern thinking with traditional values, developing complete, compassionate individuals who are ready to shape a better world.

"All you need to be happy is something to be passionate about."
Charles Kingsley · The Water-Babies, 1863

Kingsley Surf Academy

The only school in the UK with surfing in the curriculum

Surfing at Kingsley isn't an after-school activity — it's embedded into school life from Year 1. Our North Devon coastline, including Saunton, Croyde and Putsborough, provides world-class waves all year round. Students build resilience, focus and a deep connection with the natural world.

SEND Provision

The Connect Centre — CReSTeD accredited

Kingsley is the only school in Devon and Cornwall to hold CReSTeD accreditation — the gold standard for specialist SEND provision. Our purpose-built Connect Centre supports students aged 7–16 with a range of learning differences, including dyslexia, ASD, ADHD, GDD and EHCPs.

The Connect Centre is not separate from school life — it's at its heart. Mainstream students and those with additional needs thrive side by side, learning with and from one another every day.

📖
Dyslexia & SpLD
Expert support for specific learning difficulties within the mainstream curriculum.
🌟
ASD & ADHD
Calm, structured environments with specialist staff for students who need more.
💬
SALT & OT on site
Speech & Language and Occupational Therapy available within the school week.
