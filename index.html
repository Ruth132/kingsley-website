Our Ethos

Achieving success and happiness through positive mental health and wellbeing

At Kingsley, we believe the foundation of every success — academic, personal, or sporting — is a young person who feels known, valued, and supported. Our core values of Appreciate, Be Active, Connect, Give and Learn run through every part of school life.

Inspired by Charles Kingsley, a North Devon author who believed in the transformative power of a holistic education, we combine modern thinking with traditional values to develop complete, compassionate global citizens.

Curriculum

Two pathways. Infinite possibilities.

Kingsley offers a distinctive dual-pathway curriculum designed for the real world. The Core Curriculum provides a strong academic foundation across GCSE, A Level, BTEC, CTEC and ASDAN, ensuring routes to suit every ambition.

The Expansive Curriculum is where passions ignite. Delivered in specialist pathway sessions, students explore the Earth Centre, Coastal Adventures (Surf Academy), Performing Arts, STEM, and Sporting Excellence — gaining real qualifications alongside real-world experience.
