Why Surf Academy?

More than sport — a way of life

The Kingsley Surf Academy is unlike anything else in British education. We are the only school recognised by Surfing England as a School of Excellence, and the only school in the UK where surfing is woven into the curriculum from the earliest years.

Our North Devon coastline — including Saunton, Croyde and Putsborough — provides world-class conditions all year round. Students surf in all conditions, build a deep understanding of the ocean, and develop resilience, focus and freedom that carries through every part of their education.
